<h1 align="center">Quoly</h1>

<p align="center"><b>From messy supplier replies to a decision you can defend.</b></p>

<p align="center">
Quoly is the workspace for buyers: ask for quotes, let your suppliers reply the way they always do, and actually compare what comes back.
</p>

---

## The problem

You ask for quotes and chaos arrives. Emails, PDFs, Excel sheets, Word docs, datasheets — even phone photos of a price list. One has a price but no lead time. Another has a question instead of an offer. Every supplier uses their own format and their own currency.

Stitching all of that together by hand, in a spreadsheet, is slow and fragile. **Quoly organizes it for you — and the decision stays yours.**

---

## How it works

```
Create the request  →  Pick suppliers and send  →  Suppliers reply by email
→  Quoly reads it  →  You review  →  Quotes pile up
→  Compare  →  You decide (award)
```

Your suppliers **change nothing**: they keep answering by email, with no account and no portal to learn. A single request can hold several suppliers at different stages at once — one has quoted, another is still silent, another is waiting on your answer.

---

## What you can do

- Write a short commercial request and send it to the suppliers you choose.
- Let AI read every reply and turn email, PDFs, spreadsheets, docs and images into structured quote data.
- Review and correct what the AI extracted before it counts.
- Answer supplier questions with an AI-drafted reply you can use, edit, or rewrite.
- Compare every supplier side by side on the same basis, with a recommended option.
- Record who you award and why, and export the decision as a PDF.
- Keep a private directory of your suppliers, with contacts and tags.

Nothing is awarded or sent automatically. The AI proposes; you decide.

## Inside Quoly

A tour of the product, module by module.

### Home

Your starting point. A quick read of what already needs you: quotes to review, suppliers waiting on your answer, the pipeline across your requests, upcoming dates and recent activity. When nothing is pending, it says so — no busywork.

### Requests

The commercial request (RFQ) is where everything starts.

- Create one with the essentials — product, quantity, unit — and add as much or as little as you have: internal reference (shown to suppliers or kept private), Incoterm 2020 and the named place, a requested delivery date, free-text technical requirements, and an optional reference file.
- Save it as a draft and come back, or create it when the required fields are valid.
- See all your requests in one list — product, reference, status, quantity, how many quotes are already in the comparison, and which ones have a supplier waiting on you — and search across them.

### Suppliers on a request

- Add suppliers from your directory, or a one-off supplier just for this request.
- Send the request from your own mailbox, or copy it and send it yourself.
- Follow each supplier through their own stage: not contacted, awaiting response, needs your action, partial response, quote received, declined, or closed. Several stages can be true at once on the same request.

### Responses

Every supplier reply, read and understood.

- Open the original message and the files that came with it.
- See what Quoly extracted from it — commercial data, technical evidence, and any questions — all from a single read.
- A message can carry more than one meaning at once (a quote and a question together), and anything uncertain is flagged for your review.
- When a supplier asks you something, Quoly shows whether the answer is already in your request, proposes a draft reply, and lets you send it from your mailbox. You can also just mark it resolved. Nothing is sent on its own.

### Quotes

The commercial offer, structured from the supplier's own material.

- Price — including quantity breaks and tiers when the supplier priced several quantities — currency and unit, with the original figures kept and a normalized view to compare.
- MOQ when the supplier stated one, lead time split into production / transit / total only where they actually said it, plus validity and payment terms.
- Every value sits next to the supplier's original wording. Missing fields stay marked as not stated; a quote can be kept even with no price.

### Specification evidence

The technical side. When you wrote requirements, Quoly lines up the supplier's evidence against them, parameter by parameter, with a clear verdict for each (match, deviation, missing, not comparable, additional parameter, needs review). No match percentage, no score — just what lines up and what doesn't.

### Comparison

The heart of the product. Every supplier on one request, side by side.

- Commercial differences — price and tiers, currency, unit, Incoterm and place, MOQ, lead time, validity, payment terms — with anything missing left visible.
- A recommended supplier and a short written analysis, plus useful alerts (for example, when a supplier's MOQ exceeds the quantity you asked for).
- Open questions stay on the comparison, so a cheap-looking offer that's still blocked never passes as finished.

### Decision and record

You choose the supplier and record the award — an explicit action, never automatic. Write your reason, and export a one-page PDF with the figures, the comparison and your decision, ready for your records or to share.

### Quotes across requests

Find quotes without opening each request first. See which ones still need review and jump straight to the quote or its request. It's a way to find work — not a second comparison.

### Supplier directory

Your private list of suppliers. Add, search, edit, archive and restore companies; keep their contacts and mark a primary one; organize with tags. No marketplace, no outside suggestions — just the suppliers you already work with.

### Settings

Your profile and photo, light / dark / system appearance, workspace language (English or Spanish) and the default language for supplier emails, password changes, and connecting your mailbox — **Gmail or Outlook** — so Quoly can send from your inbox and pull replies back in.

## What the AI does — and what it doesn't

Quoly's AI removes the manual work: it reads messy replies, proposes the commercial structure, pulls the technical evidence, summarizes each offer, writes the comparison analysis, and drafts a reply when you ask for one.

It **does not** answer or award on its own, invent data a supplier didn't give, fill gaps so an incomplete quote looks complete, or assign a magic score. The AI proposes; you decide.
